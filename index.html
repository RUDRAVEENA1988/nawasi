Early Struggles & NSD

Born in Budhana, Muzaffarnagar (Uttar Pradesh), Nawazuddin completed his graduation in Chemistry before pursuing theatre. He joined the prestigious National School of Drama (NSD) in New Delhi, graduating in 1996 and honing an exceptional command over character nuance and voice modulation.

The Breakthrough Decade

After enduring over a decade of minuscule walk-on appearances and persistent struggle in Mumbai, 2012 marked a cinematic turning point with defining performances in Gangs of Wasseypur, Kahaani, and Talaash.

Global Acclaim

He is notably the only actor in the world to have 8 films officially selected and screened at the Cannes Film Festival, receiving accolades from prestigious bodies worldwide including International Emmy nods for Sacred Games and Serious Men.

The extraordinary true story of an impoverished labourer who carved a path through a mountain using only a hammer and chisel over 22 years in memory of his wife.
